Adds a `keda` block to the worker `defaults`, rendering a KEDA ScaledObject that scales a worker on the depth of the queues it consumes, down to zero when they are empty.
The chart derives one redis trigger per queue the instance consumes and points it at the bundled Dragonfly, and an optional Prometheus trigger holds the pods up while a diagnostic is still executing.
This replaces `autoscaling` rather than layering on it, because two autoscalers on one Deployment fight over its replica count.
